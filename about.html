Our story

Built for the 559

559eats started with one simple idea: local food deserves a local spotlight.

We’re Fresno through and through

The Central Valley has some of the most incredible food in California — from family taquerias in Southeast Fresno to craft burger joints in Clovis to Hmong-owned food trucks in the Tower District. 559eats exists to make sure all of it gets discovered.

🎉

Free for everyone

We don’t charge food trucks to be listed. Local food shouldn’t have to pay a platform just to be found.

🌎

Hyper-local focus

We only cover the Central Valley. No national chains, no LA noise. If it’s in the 559, we want it on the map.

🤝

Community-driven

Listings come from the community — from owners texting us their info to locals nominating their favorite hidden gems.

📱

Mobile-first discovery

Most people decide where to eat on their phone, standing somewhere hungry. We’re built for that moment.

The story behind 559eats

It started as a frustration: great food trucks in Fresno with no online presence, hidden gems that only regulars knew about, and no single place to find them all.

So we built one. 559eats launched as a simple directory and grew into a full discovery platform — with SMS onboarding for owners, neighborhood filters, catering connections, and a map view that actually works on mobile.

We’re proud to be a Fresno-born project, built by and for the Central Valley community.
