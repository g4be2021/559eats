🍴 Catering we bring you: 15%

When a customer books your truck for an event through 559eats and you accept it, we charge a 15% booking fee on the price you agreed with the customer (never more than 15% of the top of their budget range). You only pay on jobs you confirm. We never take a cut of your everyday sales at the window.

Common questions

Do I have to pay to be on the map?

No. The listing is free and stays free.

What is the difference between the plans?

All three make your pin glow and move you up the list. Premium has the biggest, pulsing glow, a bigger pin with your name always showing, and is never grouped into a cluster bubble. Featured has a gold glow and star. Spotlight has a softer orange glow.

How do I switch or cancel?

Log in and open Billing. Pick another plan to switch (the price difference is prorated), or tap "Cancel at end of month". You keep your plan until the month you paid for ends, then go back to the free listing automatically.

When does the 15% catering fee get charged?

Only when you confirm a catering booking that came through 559eats. You enter the price you agreed on, and 15% of it is charged right away to the card saved in your dashboard. Declined or canceled requests cost nothing.

Is my card safe?

Payments are handled by Stripe. 559eats never sees or stores your full card number.
