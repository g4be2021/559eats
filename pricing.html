Common questions
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes — cancel anytime from your owner dashboard. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your listing stays active until the end of your billing period.
How do I get listed first? ▾
Submit your free listing application and we'll review it within 24–48 hours. Once approved, you can upgrade to Featured or Sponsored at any time.
Is my payment information secure? ▾
Absolutely. Payments are processed by Stripe — the same technology used by Amazon, Google, and millions of businesses. We never see or store your card details.
When does my listing go live after paying? ▾
Immediately after payment. Your badge and placement update automatically — no waiting for manual approval.
I'm a food truck — which plan do I pick? ▾
Start with a free Basic listing to get on the map. If you want carousel placement and the Sponsored badge — which puts you front and center on the homepage — upgrade to Sponsored Truck for $49/month.
