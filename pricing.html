Common questions

Do I have to pay to be on the map?

No. The listing is free and stays free.

How do I cancel Featured?

Log in, open Billing, and tap "Cancel at end of month". You stay featured until the month you paid for ends, then go back to the free listing automatically.

When does the 15% catering fee get charged?

Only after you confirm a catering booking that came through 559eats. It is charged to the card saved in your dashboard. Declined or canceled requests cost nothing.

Is my card safe?

Payments are handled by Stripe. 559eats never sees or stores your full card number.
