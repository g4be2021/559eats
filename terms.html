5. Catering and event bookings

559eats helps customers send catering and event requests to trucks. A booking is an agreement between the customer and the truck. 559eats is not a party to it and is not a caterer.

  • Requests are not confirmed until the truck accepts. The truck sets the final menu, price, deposit and its own cancellation terms with the customer.
  • Booking fee for trucks. When a truck confirms a booking that came through 559eats, the truck pays 559eats a booking fee of 15% of the final price the truck agreed on with the customer, which the truck enters when it confirms. The fee is never more than 15% of the top of the budget range on the request. It is charged to the card saved in the owner dashboard at the time of confirming; if the charge fails, the fee is still owed. Trucks agree not to move a 559eats booking off the platform to avoid this fee.
  • Cancellations. If an event is canceled before it happens and the truck reports it within 7 days, we refund or waive the booking fee. No fee is charged for declined requests.
  • Disputes. Questions about food, service, payment or no-shows should first be raised directly between the customer and the truck. We will try to help, but we are not responsible for the outcome of an event.
