Food truck owners: text JOIN to (757) 970-3964 to get your truck listed on 559eats for free.

About this program

559eats.com is a free online directory of food trucks in Fresno, Clovis and the Central Valley, operated by TRANSGLOBAL CONNECT LLC. Our SMS program helps food truck owners get listed and keep their listing up to date.

How you opt in

You join by texting JOIN (or any message) to (757) 970-3964. We only text people who have texted us first. After you opt in, an automated assistant will ask for your business name, food type, hours and where you usually park, answer your questions, and tell you when your listing is live. You can text later to update your hours or location.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one message confirming you are unsubscribed.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us through our contact page.

Privacy

We collect the phone number you text from, the messages you send, and the business details you share (business name, food type, hours and location). We use this information only to create and maintain your 559eats listing and to reply to you.

We do not sell, rent or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Your business name, food type, hours and location will be shown publicly on 559eats.com once your listing is approved. Your phone number may be shown on your listing so customers can reach your business. If you do not want it shown, tell us and we will remove it.

We use service providers to run the program (for example, our text messaging provider and an AI service that helps write replies). They process your information only to provide these services to us.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your information? Reach us through our contact page.
